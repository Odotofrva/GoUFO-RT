Game Description

Go-UFO is a fast arcade space shooter where the player pilots a small UFO across deep space. Enemy ships, asteroids, and boss waves attack from above while the player collects upgrade cores to improve firepower, shields, speed, and special attacks.

Upgrade System

  • Rapid Fire shortens laser cooldown.
  • Spread Shot fires triple beams.
  • Shield protects from one crash.
  • Drone Wingman adds extra damage.
  • Bomb clears the current enemy wave.

Controls

  • Keyboard: Arrow keys or WASD move.
  • Space or A button fires lasers.
  • X or B button activates bomb.
  • Touch buttons work on mobile.
